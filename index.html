AuraGlobe: Migraine Atlas — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 July 2026 · Effective: 15 July 2026

This policy explains how the iOS application AuraGlobe: Migraine Atlas ("the app") handles information and treats your privacy.

1. What AuraGlobe: Migraine Atlas is

AuraGlobe: Migraine Atlas is an interactive 3D globe showing approximate, estimated adult migraine prevalence for a fixed, pre-bundled list of cities and countries, together with current weather for the place you select. It is an informational and educational reference tool. It is not a diagnostic tool, it does not provide medical advice, and it does not record, store, or process any health information about you. You cannot enter symptoms, migraine episodes, or any other personal or health data into the app, because the app has no facility to do so.

2. Personal data we collect: none

We do not collect, receive, store, sell, share, or otherwise process any personal data about you. We have no technical means of doing so: the app communicates with no server operated by or on behalf of us.

This corresponds to the "Data Not Collected" declaration on the app's App Store privacy information.

3. What the app stores on your device

The app saves a small amount of information locally on your device so it can remember your preferences between launches. This stays on your device. It is not transmitted to us, and we cannot see it.

WhatWhy
Appearance (dark/light) and temperature unit (°C/°F)To keep the app looking the way you set it
Globe options — idle spin, spin speed, clouds, starfield, sun & comet, markersTo keep the globe the way you set it
Which places you have openedTo apply the free-tier allowance and to avoid charging you twice for a place you have already opened
Whether you have seen the introductory hintSo it is not shown repeatedly
If you use Sign in with Apple: your Apple user identifier and the given name you chose to shareTo display that you are signed in, and to show your name

This information is removed when you delete the app, or at any time via Settings → Delete account inside the app (see section 8).

4. iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Sync via iCloud in the app's Profile screen, the preferences listed in section 3 — your appearance and unit settings, your globe options, the list of places you have opened, and the given name from Sign in with Apple, if any — are stored in your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so they follow you across your Apple devices.

This data resides in your iCloud account under your Apple ID. We have no access to it and cannot read, retrieve, or delete it. Your Apple user identifier is deliberately not synced. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

This feature is off unless you switch it on, and you can switch it off at any time.

5. Sign in with Apple (optional)

Signing in is entirely optional; the app is fully usable without it. If you choose to sign in, Apple provides the app with a user identifier and — only if you permit it — your given name. As stated above, these are stored on your device (and, if you enabled iCloud sync, the given name in your own iCloud). They are never sent to us.

Sign in with Apple lets you hide your email address; the app does not request your email address at all. Apple's own handling of the sign-in process is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

6. Weather data (Apple WeatherKit)

When you open a city or country in the app, the app requests current weather from Apple WeatherKit for that place.

Important: the coordinates sent are those of the place you tapped — a fixed point from a list bundled inside the app, such as the centre of Tokyo. They are not your location. The app has no location permission, never requests one, and has no ability to determine where you are.

Weather requests are made by your device directly to Apple. We do not see them. Apple's processing of WeatherKit requests is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy; attribution information is available at weatherkit.apple.com/legal-attribution.html.

7. Purchases

AuraGlobe: Migraine Atlas offers an optional paid upgrade. All purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using Apple's In-App Purchase system.

  • We never receive or have access to your payment card details, billing address, or Apple ID credentials.
  • Your purchase is verified on your device using Apple's cryptographically signed receipts. No purchase information is sent to us.
  • Restoring purchases is handled by Apple against your Apple ID.

Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy. Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous sales reports that do not identify individual users.

8. Retention and deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side.

Data held on your device or in your iCloud is under your control:

  • Settings → Delete account inside the app erases the locally stored information listed in section 3 and also clears the synced copy from your iCloud.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its local data from your device.
  • You can manage or remove iCloud data through your device's iCloud settings.

9. What we never do

  • We do not track you, across this app or any other app or website.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting services.
  • The app contains no third-party software development kits.
  • We do not use cookies or device advertising identifiers (IDFA).
  • We do not build profiles of users, and we carry out no automated decision-making.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act. We never have.

10. Links to other services

The app contains links to external websites — for example data-source credits (GeoNames, Solar System Scope, GitHub), Apple's weather attribution page, and an App Store listing for another app by the same developer. Opening a link leaves AuraGlobe: Migraine Atlas and opens your browser or the App Store. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, and this policy does not apply to them.

11. Children's privacy

AuraGlobe: Migraine Atlas is a general-audience reference app and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it collects no personal data from children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

12. Your rights

Privacy laws including the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) give you rights over personal data that a company holds about you — such as access, correction, deletion, portability, and objection.

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export, or erase, and no data for you to opt out of being sold or shared. Any data described in this policy sits on your own device or in your own iCloud account, where you already control it directly (see section 8).

If you believe we hold data about you and wish to exercise a right, contact us at rakukoli65@gmail.com and we will respond within the period required by applicable law. If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

13. International users

The app is available worldwide. As we collect no personal data, we carry out no international transfers of personal data. Data you keep in iCloud is stored and transferred by Apple in accordance with Apple's own policies.

14. Security

Data stored on your device is protected by iOS and your device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID. Data stored in your iCloud account is protected by Apple's security measures and your Apple ID credentials. Because we operate no servers and hold no data, there is no database of ours that could be breached.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app changes — for example if a future version adds a feature that handles data differently. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the current version, and the current policy will always be available at this address. Material changes will be reflected here before or when the relevant app update is released. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
